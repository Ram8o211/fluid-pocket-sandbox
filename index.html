Particle volume radius controls the excluded/packing radius: smaller values allow more interpenetration and compact the same mass; larger values make the same mass occupy more volume. Density remains a separate property used for buoyancy and stratification.

Crystallinity controls lattice ordering/faceting. Solid subdivision controls whether the solid behaves as one coherent body or as separate grains/sand.

One finger uses the selected tool. Two fingers always control the camera; pinch to zoom.

2D supports a much larger workspace. Mold objects are open at the top so liquid can be poured in and frozen into a low-subdivision solid.

Combustion threshold is independent of melting and boiling points. Burning consumes matter and deterministically creates 2–3 daughter materials while conserving mass.
